Day by day

The itinerary, unfolded.

Use this itinerary as a planning guide. Confirm current departure dates, access conditions and availability before booking.

DAY
01
SPIRITUAL CULTURE

Arrive in Varanasi

Arrive at the airport or railway station and transfer to the hotel. After rest, visit the Ganges for Ganga Aarti and experience the spiritual atmosphere of the ghats.

  • Varanasi ghats
  • Ganga Aarti

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY
02
RIVER • TEMPLE

Varanasi & the Ganges

Take an early-morning boat ride on the Ganges and see the famous ghats. Later visit Kashi Vishwanath Corridor and Temple, with Abhishek if intended, followed by Bharat Mata Temple and Banaras Hindu University. Explore local cuisine in the evening.

  • Ganges boat ride
  • Varanasi ghats
  • Kashi Vishwanath Corridor and Temple
  • Bharat Mata Temple
  • Banaras Hindu University

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY
03
BUDDHIST HERITAGE

Varanasi – Sarnath – Varanasi

After breakfast, take an excursion to Sarnath, where Buddha delivered his first sermon. Visit Buddha temples, Dhamekha Stupa, Chaukhandi Stupa and the Archaeological Museum. Return to Varanasi; time for market shopping.

  • Sarnath
  • Dhamekha Stupa
  • Chaukhandi Stupa
  • Archaeological Museum

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY
04
CRAFT • CUISINE • TEMPLES

Markets, Silk & Temples

Walk through Banaras markets and visit a silk factory. Continue to Annapurna Temple, Sankat Mochan Temple, Manas Mandir and Kaal Bhairav Temple. Attend Ganga Aarti again and enjoy local chaat and lassi.

  • Banaras markets and silk factory
  • Annapurna Temple
  • Sankat Mochan Temple
  • Manas Mandir
  • Kaal Bhairav Temple
  • Ganga Aarti

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY
05
DEPARTURE

Varanasi – Return

After breakfast, check out and transfer to the railway station or airport for the return journey.

  • Airport / Railway Station
