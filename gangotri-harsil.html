Itinerary & package details

Gangotri – Harsil Valley

Duration : 09 Days Ex – Haridwar

Route : Haridwar – Uttarkashi – Dharali – Gangotri – Rishikesh – Haridwar

Itinerary :

Day 01: Arrival at Haridwar – Rest Day

Reach Haridwar & transfer to the stay facility. Check-in, freshen up & relax. If time permits,

visit Har Ki Pauri for Ganga Aarti in the evening. Walk around local markets or ghats. Return

to the stay facility, have dinner and overnight stay in Haridwar.

Day 02: Haridwar to Dharali/Harsil via Uttarkashi (Aprox 270 km, 9–10 hours by road)

This morning, rise early and depart to Dharali (Harsil). Have breakfast enroute at a

convenient place. Drive further to Dharali. Visit Kashi Vishwanath Temple at Uttarkashi. An

ancient Shiva temple with historical and spiritual importance. Arrival at Dharali By evening.

Check into the stay facility & fresh up. Later, if time permits, visit the Kalpakedar Temple

located on the banks of the Bhagirathi. Attend evening Aarti at the temple. Return to the

stay facility, have dinner and overnight stay at Dharali.

Day 03: Trekking – Dharali → Bhagori → Lama Top → Harsil → Back

This morning, after breakfast, we leave for a full day short trekking excursion. Trek to

Bhagori Village, Lama Top and Harsil. Bhagori Village is a Tibetan village known for wooden

houses and hospitality. Lama Top offers panoramic views of snow-capped peaks and valley

below. Harsil is Army Headquarter of the region. Evening, Trek or drive back to Dharali.

Return to the stay facility, have dinner and overnight stay at Dharali.

Day 04: Gartang Gali Trek + Nelong Valley + Gangotri Darshan

This morning, rise early and drive towards Gartang Gali (Aaprox 25 km from Dharali).

Gartang Gali is An ancient wooden bridge and cliff-hugging path – 2.5 km (one-way)

moderate trek, originally used for Indo-Tibetan Trade. Later, proceed to Nelong Valley which

is a restricted valley near the Indo-China border (depends on Permit from Army). Enroute,

stop at Bhairav Ghati, and further drive to Gangotri. Darshan at Gangotri Temple, one of the

Chardhams in Uttarakhand. Gangotri is located at an altitude of 3,100m and is situated on

the banks of Bhagirathi river. Late afternoon we proceed back to Dharali. Late evening,

return to the stay facility, have dinner and overnight stay at Dharali.

Day 05: Saat Taal Trek

This morning, rise early and leave for a Trekking Excursion to Saat Taal, a group of 7

high-altitude lakes. It is a Moderate to difficult trek & takes 6–7 hours round trip. We have

Pack Breakfast and Pack Lunch during the trek. Late afternoon, return to Dharali. Evening,

Visit the Kalpkedar Temple located on the banks of Bhagirathi. Attend the evening Aarti at

Kalpakedar Temple. Return to the stay facility, have dinner and overnight stay at Dharali.

Day06: Mukhba Village and Mandakini Waterfall

This morning, after breakfast, we leave for a full day short trekking excursion to Mukhba

Village, the winter seat of Goddess Ganga. When Gangotri Temple is closed during winters,

the utsav murti is brought at Mukhba and worshipped here. Have a look at the traditional

wooden houses and explore the beautiful heritage village. Later, head back to Dharali.

Afternoon, we take a walk to the Mandakini Waterfall. It is a scenic, lesser-known waterfall

near Dharali. Later, return to the stay facility, have dinner and overnight stay at Dharali.

Day 07: Dharali to Rishikesh

This morning, rise early & check out. Drive to Rishikesh (270 km, 9–10 hours). If time

permits, go for a short white-water rafting session (as per time availability). Reach the stay

facility and check in. Evening, attend the Ganga Aarti at Rishikesh. Return to the stay facility,

have dinner and overnight stay at Rishikesh.

Day 08: Rishikesh – Haridwar

This Morning is free for leisure / adventure activities like Water Rafting / Bunjee Jumping or

Visit to Neelkanth Mahadev Temple. These activities are directly payable and are as per

choice. Afternoon we depart to Haridwar. Reach and check into the stay facility. Later the

time is free to explore Haridwar on your own or visit Haridwar Markets for Shopping. If

interested you can attend the evening Ganga Aarti at Hari ki Pauri. Later, return to the stay

facility, have dinner and overnight stay at Haridwar.

Day09: Haridwar – Return

This morning, Departure to Delhi in the morning by Janashatabdi express, further board train

to C. Sambhaji Nagar / Pune
