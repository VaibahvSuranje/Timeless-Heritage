Day by day

The itinerary, unfolded.

Use this itinerary as a planning guide. Confirm current departure dates, access conditions and availability before booking.

DAY
01
PALACES • TEMPLES

Arrival in Gwalior

Arrive in Gwalior and begin with the city’s palaces and temple architecture, followed by shopping and local food in the evening.

  • Jai Vilas Palace
  • Teli Ka Mandir
  • Sas Bahu Temple
  • Shopping and local food

Overnight: Gwalior

DAY
02
CITY HERITAGE

Gwalior Sightseeing

Explore Gwalior’s Sun Temple and Gujari Mahal Museum. Time for local markets and handicraft shopping.

  • Sun Temple Gwalior
  • Gujari Mahal Museum
  • Local markets

Overnight: Gwalior

DAY
03
FORTS • TEMPLES

Gwalior – Orchha – Khajuraho

Depart for Orchha and visit the Orchha Fort Complex, Raja Mahal, Chaturbhuj Temple and Ram Raja Temple before continuing to Khajuraho.

  • Orchha Fort Complex
  • Raja Mahal
  • Chaturbhuj Temple
  • Ram Raja Temple

Overnight: Khajuraho

DAY
04
TEMPLE ARCHITECTURE

Khajuraho Sightseeing

Visit the Western, Eastern and Southern Groups of Temples. The Khajuraho Dance Festival site area and Light & Sound Show are subject to schedule.

  • Western Group of Temples
  • Eastern Group of Temples
  • Southern Group of Temples
  • Dance Festival site / Light & Sound Show (subject to schedule)

Overnight: Khajuraho

DAY
05
NATURE • WILDLIFE

Khajuraho Excursion

Visit Raneh Falls and Ken Gharial Sanctuary. An optional safari at Panna Tiger Reserve is listed in the itinerary.

  • Raneh Falls
  • Ken Gharial Sanctuary
  • Panna Tiger Reserve safari (optional)

Overnight: Khajuraho

DAY
06
DEPARTURE

Departure

The source itinerary specifies drop at Gwalior. Please confirm the transfer timing and departure arrangements when enquiring.

  • Gwalior drop
