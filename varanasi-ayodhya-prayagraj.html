Day by day

The itinerary, unfolded.

Use this itinerary as a planning guide. Confirm current departure dates, access conditions and availability before booking.

DAY
01
ARRIVAL • AARTI

Arrive in Varanasi

Pick-up from Varanasi airport or railway station and transfer to the hotel. In the evening visit Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat and Durga Kund Temple.

  • Dashashwamedh Ghat
  • Ganga Aarti
  • Durga Kund Temple

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY
02
SPIRITUAL HERITAGE

Varanasi Local Sightseeing

Take an early boat ride on the Ganges and see Manikarnika, Dashashwamedh, Assi and other ghats. Visit Kashi Vishwanath, Annapurna and Kaal Bhairav temples, Banaras Hindu University and Sarnath. Return to the hotel.

  • Ganges boat ride
  • Kashi Vishwanath Temple
  • Annapurna Temple
  • Kaal Bhairav Temple
  • Banaras Hindu University
  • Sarnath

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY
03
SACRED CITY

Varanasi to Ayodhya

After breakfast, depart for Ayodhya. After check-in and rest, visit Hanuman Garhi, Ram Janmabhoomi, Kanak Bhawan and Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Nyas as listed in the source.

  • Hanuman Garhi
  • Ram Janmabhoomi
  • Kanak Bhawan

Overnight: Ayodhya

DAY
04
TEMPLE HERITAGE

Ayodhya Local

Full-day sightseeing after breakfast, including Ram Ki Paidi, Tulsi Udyan, Tulasi Smarak Bhavan, Naya Ghat, Shri Sita Ram Mandir and Mani Parvat Temple.

  • Ram Ki Paidi
  • Tulsi Udyan
  • Tulasi Smarak Bhavan
  • Naya Ghat
  • Shri Sita Ram Mandir
  • Mani Parvat Temple

Overnight: Ayodhya

DAY
05
RIVER CONFLUENCE

Ayodhya to Prayagraj

Transfer to Prayagraj after breakfast. Visit Triveni Sangam, with time for a holy bath or rituals if desired, followed by Allahabad Fort–Patalpuri Temple, Bade Hanuman Ji Temple, Alopshankri Temple, Anand Bhawan and Azad Park.

  • Triveni Sangam
  • Allahabad Fort – Patalpuri Temple
  • Bade Hanuman Ji Temple
  • Alopshankri Temple
  • Anand Bhawan
  • Azad Park

Overnight: Prayagraj

DAY
06
RETURN ROUTE

Prayagraj – Varanasi

After breakfast, check out and enjoy free time in Prayagraj. After lunch, depart for Varanasi. If interested, attend evening Ganga Aarti.

  • Prayagraj free time
  • Ganga Aarti (if interested)

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY
07
DEPARTURE

Varanasi – Return

After breakfast, check out and transfer to the airport for the return journey.

  • Varanasi Airport
