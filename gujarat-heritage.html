Day by day

The itinerary, unfolded.

Use this itinerary as a planning guide. Confirm current departure dates, access conditions and availability before booking.

DAY
01
HERITAGE • TEXTILES • CITY CULTURE

Arrival & Ahmedabad Heritage

Arrive in Ahmedabad and settle in before beginning the journey through the city’s heritage.

  • Sabarmati Ashram
  • Kankariya Lake & Zoo
  • Sabarmati Riverfront

Overnight: Ahmedabad

DAY
02
ARCHAEOLOGY • ANCIENT CIVILISATION

Ahmedabad – Lothal – Ahmedabad

Travel to Lothal, an important archaeological site associated with the Harappan civilisation. Explore the excavated ruins and museum, followed by local experiences around the site. Continue to nearby Uthellya and its ruins before returning.

  • Lothal archaeological site
  • Lothal museum
  • Uthellya ruins

Overnight: Ahmedabad

DAY
03
ARCHITECTURE • CRAFTSMANSHIP

Modhera & Patan

Explore the Modhera Sun Temple complex and its iconic stepwell before continuing towards Patan. Discover Rani ki Vav and the Patan Patola Heritage Museum.

  • Modhera Sun Temple
  • Rani ki Vav
  • Patan Patola Heritage Museum

Overnight: Ahmedabad

DAY
04
JAIN ARCHITECTURE • STEPWELL HERITAGE

Discover More of Ahmedabad

Continue exploring Ahmedabad’s architectural and cultural heritage.

  • Hutheesing Jain Temple
  • Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel National Memorial
  • Adalaj Stepwell
  • Bai Harir Vav
  • Leisure time around Law Garden

Overnight: Ahmedabad

DAY
05
CITY CULTURE • DEPARTURE

Ahmedabad & Departure

The final day gives you time for last-minute shopping and sightseeing before departure.

  • Ahmedabad street and cultural experiences
