Itinerary & package details

Madurai – Rameshwaram

03 Nights / 04 Days

Itinerary :

Day 01 : Madurai

Arrive at Madurai Airport and transfer to a hotel in Madurai. Check-in at the hotel. After fresh

up go for sightseeing, visit Meenakshi Amman temple & Tiruparakkundram temple. Later

come back to the hotel for overnight stay.

Day 02 : Madurai – Rameshwaram

This morning, post breakfast depart to Rameshwaram (175 Km / 4 Hours). Enroute Visit

Pamban Bridge. Reach Rameshwaram and Check into the Hotel. Fresh up and later visit

Ramanathaswamy Temple. Evening is Free at Leisure. Overnight Stay in Rameshwaram.

Day 03 : Rameshwaram

Morning, after breakfast leave for Rameshwaram Sightseeing. Visit Madhana Parvathi

Temple, Agnitheertham, Ramjharoka Temple, Dhanushkodi and Nala Sethu (Ram Sethu).

Later time is free at the Rameshwaram Market. Overnight stay at Rameshwaram.

Day 04 : Rameshwaram – Madurai

Morning, post breakfast, leave for Madurai. Reach Madurai Airport to board a flight for

Return Journey.
