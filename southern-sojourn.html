Itinerary & package details

Southern Sojourn

Duration : 04 Nights / 05 Days

Highlights : Ranganathaswamy Temple, Jambukeshwar Temple, Brihadrishwarar Temple,

Gangaikonda Chola Puram

Itinerary :

Day 1: Arrival & Core Trichy

Arrive in Trichy Airport. Pick up and transfer to your hotel. Later visit Sri Ranganathaswamy

Temple in Srirangam, one of the largest functioning temple complexes in the world. Late

evening, return to the stay facility and overnight Stay in Trichy.

Day 2: Elements of Water & Ancient Engineering

Morning, post breakfast, Visit the serene Jambukeswarar Temple in Thiruvanaikaval, a

Pancha Bhoota Sthalam dedicated to the water element, where an underground spring

naturally flows through the sub-shrine. Afternoon take a short 20 km drive out to the Kallanai

Dam (Grand Anicut). Built by the Cholas in the 2nd century AD, it stands as one of the oldest

water-diversion structures still in use today. Evening, return to the stay facility and overnight

Stay in Trichy.

Day 3: Chola Grandeur in Thanjavur

Morning, post breakfast, Check out of your Trichy hotel and drive roughly 1.5 hours (60 km)

to Thanjavur. Reach and Check into the Hotel. Afternoon, visit the Thanjavur Maratha

Palace Complex, dropping by the ancient manuscripts section of the Saraswathi Mahal

Library and the Chola bronzes at the Art Gallery. Spend your evening at the spectacular,

UNESCO-listed Brihadeeswarar Temple (Big Temple), marveling at its massive monolithic

Nandi and towering vimana. Late evening return to the stay facility and overnight Stay in

Thanjavur.

Day 4: Day Trip to Living Chola Temples

Morning, post breakfast, drive from Thanjavur to the Darasuram to see the Airavatesvara

Temple, famous for its stone chariot architecture and intricate miniature carvings. Enroute

also visit the Gajendra Varada Perumal Temple at Kabisthalam which is famous for the

Gajendra Moksha Story mentioned in the 8th Skanda of Bhagvat Purana. Later, Continue to

Gangaikonda Cholapuram, built to mimic the Big Temple but with more delicate, feminine

contours. Evening, return to the stay facility and overnight Stay in Thanjavur.

Day 5: Spiritual Closure & Departure

Morning: Head back toward Trichy. On the outskirts, make a stop at the highly revered

Samayapuram Mariamman Temple to see the powerful local deity. Later proceed to Thrichy

Airport to Board a flight for return Journey.
