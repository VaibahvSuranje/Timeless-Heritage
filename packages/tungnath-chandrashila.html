Day by day

The itinerary, unfolded.

Use this itinerary as a planning guide. Confirm current departure dates, access conditions and availability before booking.

DAY
01
SPIRITUAL CULTURE

Arrive in Haridwar

Arrive at Haridwar and report to the stay facility. As time permits, visit ashrams and temples. In the evening, visit Hari ki Pauri for Ganga Aarti.

  • Haridwar temples and ashrams
  • Hari ki Pauri

Overnight: Haridwar

DAY
02
HIMALAYAN ROUTE

Haridwar – Ukhimath

Depart early for Ukhimath. Stop at Devprayag, where Alaknanda and Bhagirathi meet, and at Rudraprayag viewpoint. On arrival, check in and visit Ukhimath Temple, the winter seat of Kedarnath.

  • Devprayag viewpoint
  • Rudraprayag viewpoint
  • Ukhimath Temple

Overnight: Ukhimath

DAY
03
TREKKING • TEMPLE

Chopta – Tungnath – Chandrashila

Drive to Chopta, the trek base. Trek to Tungnath for darshan, then continue towards Chandrashila Peak for Himalayan views. Descend to Chopta and return to Ukhimath.

  • Chopta
  • Tungnath Temple
  • Chandrashila Peak

Overnight: Ukhimath

DAY
04
LAKE • MOUNTAIN VIEWS

Deoriatal & Rishikesh

Leave for Sari and trek to Deoriatal, with views of Himalayan ranges. Return to Ukhimath by noon, then travel to Rishikesh. Visit Lakshman Jhula, 13 Manzila Mandir and other spots as time permits, then drive to Haridwar.

  • Sari village
  • Deoriatal
  • Lakshman Jhula
  • 13 Manzila Mandir

Overnight: Haridwar

DAY
05
DEPARTURE

Haridwar – Return

Option to attend morning Ganga Aarti at Hari ki Pauri. After breakfast, drop at Haridwar bus stand or station for the return journey.

  • Hari ki Pauri
  • Haridwar Bus Stand / Station
