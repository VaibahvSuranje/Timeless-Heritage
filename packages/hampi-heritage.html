Day by day

The itinerary, unfolded.

Use this itinerary as a planning guide. Confirm current departure dates, access conditions and availability before booking.

DAY
01
RIVERSIDE RUINS

Arrival & Riverside Ruins

Arrive at Hosapete, transfer to Hampi and check in. After breakfast, explore Hampi Bazaar and Virupaksha Temple, stroll along Tungabhadra River ghats and climb Hemakuta Hill for sunset.

  • Hampi Bazaar
  • Virupaksha Temple
  • Tungabhadra River ghats
  • Hemakuta Hill

Overnight: Hampi

DAY
02
UNESCO HERITAGE

The Heart of the Empire

Visit the Vijaya Vittala Temple complex, famous for its stone chariot and musical pillars. Explore the Royal Enclosure, Queen’s Bath, Lotus Mahal and Elephant Stables. After lunch, visit Hazara Rama Temple, Ugra Narasimha, Badavi Lingam and Matanga Hill for sunset.

  • Vijaya Vittala Temple
  • Royal Enclosure
  • Queen’s Bath
  • Lotus Mahal
  • Elephant Stables
  • Hazara Rama Temple
  • Ugra Narasimha
  • Badavi Lingam
  • Matanga Hill

Overnight: Hampi

DAY
03
VILLAGE • RIVER

Anegundi Village & Riverside Adventures

Take a coracle ferry across the Tungabhadra River to Anegundi village. Visit Anjanadri Hill (Monkey Temple), believed to be Lord Hanuman’s birthplace. After lunch, relax or consider seasonal cliff jumping at Sanapur Lake; a coracle ride may also be available. Explore local cafés on the Anegundi side.

  • Anegundi village
  • Anjanadri Hill
  • Sanapur Lake (seasonal activities)

Overnight: Hampi

DAY
04
HIDDEN HERITAGE

Hidden Gems & Departure

Explore Achyutaraya Temple and nearby riverside ruins. In the afternoon, visit the Archaeological Museum in Kamalapura or shop for souvenirs, then head to Hospet for the return bus journey.

  • Achyutaraya Temple
  • Riverside ruins
  • Archaeological Museum, Kamalapura
  • Hospet
