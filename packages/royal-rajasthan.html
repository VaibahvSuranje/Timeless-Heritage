Itinerary & package details

Royal Rajasthan

Duration : 08 Nights / 09 Days

Route : Jaipur – Pushkar – Jodhpur – Udaipur – Kumbhalgarh

Itinerary :

Day 01 : Arrive Jaipur

Today, arrive at Jaipur. Pick up from the Airport / Railway Station & proceed to the Stay

Facility. Check in & Fresh up. Evening is free at Leisure. Have Dinner and Overnight stay at

Jaipur. (D)

Day 02 : Jaipur

This Morning, rise early, have breakfast and leave for a full day local sightseeing. Today You

cover : Jantar Mantar, City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Albert Hall, Birla Mandir, Statue Circle,

Gaitore Ki Chhatriyan crematorium. Late Afternoon visit Amer Fort. Late evening also enjoy

the Sound and Light show at the fort(Ticket at Own Cost). Drive back to the stay facility.

Have dinner & Overnight stay is in Jaipur.

(B/D)

Day 03 : Jaipur to Pushkar

This Morning, rise early and depart to Pushkar. The distance is approx. 142 km & it takes

roughly around 3 & 1/2 Hours of Drive. Reach Pushkar & Check into the stay facility. Later

we proceed for Pushkar Sightseeing. First we visit the Pushkar Lake & Brahma Temple. We

then proceed to Savitri Temple, Man Mahal Palace & Pushkar Bazaar. Drive back to the stay

facility. Have dinner & Overnight stay is in Pushkar.

(B/D)

Day 04 : Pushkar to Jodhpur

Morning Post Breakfast, leave for Jodhpur. Reach Jodhpur, Check into the Hotel. Time is

free for Rest. Post Lunch, visit the Umaid Bhavan Palace. Evening Return to the hotel. Have

dinner & Overnight stay at Jodhpur.

(B/D)

Day 05 : Jodhpur

Morning, post breakfast, we leave for full day Sightseeing. Visit Jaswant Thada. Post Lunch

drive to Mehrangarh Fort. Explore the fort and enjoy a classic sunset from atop the fort. Late

evening, return to the Hotel, have dinner and Overnight Stay at Jodhpur.

(B/D)

Day 06 : Jodhpur to Udaipur via Nathdwara

This Morning, after breakfast, depart to Udaipur. Enroute, Visit the Shrinath ji Temple at

Nathdwara. Also visit Rajsamand Lake enroute. Evening, reach Udaipur & Check into the

stay facility. Later, have dinner & Overnight stay is in Udaipur.

(B/D)

Day 07 : Udaipur Local

This Morning, after breakfast, we leave for full day of Udaipur Local Sightseeing. Today you

cover : Udaipur City Palace, Lake Pichola (Boat Ride at Own Cost), Bagore ki Haveli,

Jagdish Temple & Fateh Sagar Lake. Late evening, return to the Hotel, have dinner and

Overnight Stay at Udaipur.

(B/D)

Day 08 : Udaipur – Kumbhalgarh – Udaipur

This Morning, after breakfast, depart to Kumbhalgarh. The distance is approx. 85 km & it

takes roughly around 2 Hours of Drive. Also a UNESCO World Heritage Site, the current

Kumbhalgarh Fort was commissioned and built by Rana Kumbha. One of the most

interesting facts about the fort is that, the walls of the fort extend over 38 km and is the

second longest wall after Great Wall of China. The fort also has a number of Hindu & Jain

Temples inside its Complex. Late afternoon, proceed back to Udaipur. Reach back, have

dinner & Overnight stay is in Udaipur.

(B/D)

Day 09 : Udaipur

This Morning, after breakfast, morning time is free for shopping. Later, check out from the

hotel & drop at the Abu Road Railway Station / Ahmedabad Railway Station or Airport to

board a flight for return Journey.

(B)
