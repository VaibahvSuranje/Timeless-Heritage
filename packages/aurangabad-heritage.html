Arrival & City Heritage

  • Soneri Mahal

DAY
02
CAVES • TEMPLE

Grishneshwar & Ellora Caves

After breakfast, visit Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga and Ellora Caves, known locally as Verul and designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Explore the listed caves: Cave 10 Vishwakarma / Carpenter’s Cave; Cave 11 three-storeyed monastery with Vajrayana iconography; Cave 15 Dashavatara; Cave 16 Kailasa Temple; Cave 21 Rameshwar; Cave 29 Dhumar Lena; Cave 30 Chota Kailash; Cave 32 Indra Sabha; Cave 33 Jagannath Sabha.

  • Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga
  • Ellora Caves 10, 11, 15, 16, 21, 29, 30, 32 and 33

Overnight: Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar

03

Forts & Caves

  • Deogiri / Daulatabad Fort
  • Bhadra Maruti Mandir, Khultabad
  • Aurangabad Caves

Overnight: Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar
