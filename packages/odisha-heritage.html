Day by day

The itinerary, unfolded.

Use this itinerary as a planning guide. Confirm current departure dates, access conditions and availability before booking.

DAY
01
ARRIVAL

Arrival in Bhubaneswar

Arrive at Bhubaneswar airport, transfer to the hotel and spend the evening at leisure. Dinner and overnight stay in Bhubaneswar.

Overnight: Bhubaneswar

DAY
02
ANCIENT HERITAGE

Bhubaneswar Caves

After breakfast, visit the twin hills of Khandagiri and Udayagiri, including Jain temple and cave sites such as Elephant Cave, Lion Cave and Queen Cave.

  • Khandagiri
  • Udayagiri
  • Jain caves

Overnight: Bhubaneswar

DAY
03
TEMPLES • COASTAL CULTURE

Bhubaneswar – Konark – Puri

Drive towards Puri via Dhauli, Pipli and the famous Konark Sun Temple. Continue to Puri and visit Jagannath Temple (the source notes non-Hindus are not allowed) or relax on Puri’s golden beach.

  • Dhauli
  • Pipli
  • Konark Sun Temple
  • Jagannath Temple or Puri beach

Overnight: Puri

DAY
04
LAGOON • NATURE

Puri – Chilika – Puri

Drive to Satapada, where Chilika Lake meets the Bay of Bengal, and visit the Irrawaddy Dolphin Sanctuary. Boat ride in Chilika Lagoon is at own cost. Return to Puri.

  • Satapada
  • Chilika Lagoon
  • Irrawaddy Dolphin Sanctuary

Overnight: Puri

DAY
05
TEMPLE ARCHITECTURE

Puri – Bhubaneswar

Check out and depart for Bhubaneswar. Visit the city’s temple circuit, with temples constructed between the 7th and 13th centuries.

  • Lingaraj Temple
  • Mukteswar Temple
  • Parsuram Temple
  • Rajarani Temple
  • Kedargouri Temple
  • Bindusagar Lake

Overnight: Bhubaneswar

DAY
06
DEPARTURE

Bhubaneswar – Airport Drop

After breakfast, check out and transfer to Bhubaneswar airport for the return journey.

  • Bhubaneswar Airport
