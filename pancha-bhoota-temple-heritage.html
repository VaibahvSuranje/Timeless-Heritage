Day by day

The itinerary, unfolded.

Use this itinerary as a planning guide. Confirm current departure dates, access conditions and availability before booking.

DAY
01
TEMPLE • TEXTILES

Chennai – Srikalahasti – Kanchipuram

Early-morning pickup from Chennai. Visit Srikalahasteeswara Temple, with optional Rahu-Ketu Pooja, then Ekambareswarar, Kamakshi Amman and Varadharaja Perumal temples. Time for silk saree shopping.

  • Srikalahasteeswara Temple
  • Ekambareswarar Temple
  • Kamakshi Amman Temple
  • Varadharaja Perumal Temple
  • Silk saree shopping

Overnight: Kanchipuram

DAY
02
SACRED LANDSCAPE

Kanchipuram – Tiruvannamalai

Visit Arunachaleswarar Temple for darshan, with optional Girivalam path. Visit Ramana Maharshi Ashram and Skandashram viewpoint subject to time; spend the evening around Arunachala Hill.

  • Arunachaleswarar Temple
  • Girivalam path (optional)
  • Ramana Maharshi Ashram
  • Skandashram viewpoint (subject to time)

Overnight: Tiruvannamalai

DAY
03
TEMPLE ARCHITECTURE

Tiruvannamalai – Trichy

Travel to Trichy and visit Jambukeswarar Temple, Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple, Rockfort Temple and local markets and temple surroundings.

  • Jambukeswarar Temple
  • Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple
  • Rockfort Temple
  • Local markets

Overnight: Trichy

DAY
04
ARCHITECTURE • CRAFT

Trichy – Tanjore

Visit Brihadeeswarar Temple, Thanjavur Palace, Saraswathi Mahal Library and Art Gallery. Explore traditional handicrafts and Tanjore paintings.

  • Brihadeeswarar Temple
  • Thanjavur Palace
  • Saraswathi Mahal Library
  • Art Gallery
  • Traditional handicrafts

Overnight: Tanjore

DAY
05
SACRED HERITAGE

Tanjore – Chidambaram

Visit Gangai Konda Cholapuram en route, then Nataraja Temple, temple darshan and Chidambara Rahasyam. Optional Pichavaram boating, subject to time.

  • Gangai Konda Cholapuram
  • Nataraja Temple
  • Chidambara Rahasyam
  • Pichavaram boating (optional, subject to time)

Overnight: Chidambaram

DAY
06
DEPARTURE

Chidambaram – Chennai

Visit Thillai Kali Amman Temple and travel along the scenic East Coast Road. Arrive in Chennai for drop at airport, railway station or residence.

  • Thillai Kali Amman Temple
  • East Coast Road
  • Chennai drop
