Booking Information

Learn how to enquire about a Timeless Heritage journey and what to confirm before booking.

Draft for business review. This page contains editable placeholder wording and has not been legally reviewed. Confirm it reflects the actual business before publication.

1. Choose a journey

Explore the package collection and open the itinerary that interests you.

2. Enquire on WhatsApp

Use the package’s Book Now button or the general enquiry form. The form opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message; you choose whether to send it.

3. Confirm current details

Ask the team to confirm upcoming departure dates, availability, final price, inclusions, exclusions, accommodation and transport details for your chosen itinerary.

4. Confirm your booking

An enquiry or WhatsApp message does not itself confirm a booking. Ask for the booking confirmation and applicable payment, cancellation and refund terms.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get the full itinerary? Contact the team through WhatsApp. How do I check upcoming dates? Ask the team; old dates in source brochures are not treated as current departures. Are flights included? This varies by package; check that package’s inclusion list. Are monument tickets included? The package exclusions should be reviewed; ask the team if unclear. Can I request a customized trip? Yes, send a general enquiry to discuss your destination and preferences.
